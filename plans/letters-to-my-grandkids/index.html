Letters to My Grandkids

Guided memory books that help older folks write their life stories.

Who it's for

An older adult, or the adult child of one, who wants to get a lifetime of stories, lessons and love onto paper before they're lost.

Why it's meaningful

Every family has stories that disappear when someone passes. A memory book lets grandparents tell their grandkids who they really were: the first job, the hard years, the faith that carried them. It's a gift that can't be bought anywhere else.

What you'd make or do

Guided memory books: a set of gentle questions, one per page, that help someone write their life story a little at a time. Offer a printable book, a printed version, and later a 'we interview you' option.

Your first 30 days

  1. Week 1: Write 52 guided questions (one a week for a year), from childhood to advice for the grandkids.
  2. Week 2: Use AI to design the memory book layout and a simple website.
  3. Week 3: Give 3 free copies to friends or families and ask them to try the first few pages.
  4. Week 4: Improve the questions they got stuck on, and write a short gift page for the holidays.

How you'd find people

Grandparent and retiree groups, senior centers, churches, and adult children shopping for meaningful gifts around Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day.

A sample AI prompt

Paste this into Grok Bot or any AI builder.

Help me build Letters to My Grandkids, a guided memory book that helps older adults write their life stories. Write 52 warm, easy questions grouped by life stage (childhood, young adult, work, family, faith and lessons, letters to the grandkids). Then draft a simple, gentle one-page website explaining the book. Plain words, big type, no income claims.
