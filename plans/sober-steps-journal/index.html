Sober Steps Journal

Devotionals and daily journals for people in their first year of sobriety.

Who it's for

Someone in recovery, a few years along, who remembers how hard the first year was and wants to walk beside people who are just starting.

Why it's meaningful

The first year of sobriety can be lonely and raw. A daily page of encouragement, prayer or reflection can be a small handrail on a hard day. Your story becomes someone else's hope.

What you'd make or do

Daily devotionals and guided journals for the first year of sobriety: printable or printed journals with a short reflection, a prompt and a check-in for each day. Faith-based or not, depending on your foundation.

Your first 30 days

  1. Week 1: Write the first 30 daily pages in your own voice, with AI helping you shape and edit them.
  2. Week 2: Use AI to lay them out as a clean printable journal and create a simple website.
  3. Week 3: Share the first 30 days free with a few people in recovery (with care and permission) and ask what helped.
  4. Week 4: Revise, then plan the full 365-day journal in monthly volumes.

How you'd find people

Recovery-friendly churches, sober communities online, counselors and sponsors who might recommend it, and honest posts about your own journey if you're comfortable sharing.

A sample AI prompt

Paste this into Grok Bot or any AI builder.

Help me create a 30-day guided journal called Sober Steps for people in their first year of sobriety. For each day, write a short encouraging reflection (under 120 words), one journaling prompt and a simple daily check-in. Tone: gentle, honest, hopeful, never preachy. Offer an optional faith-based version with a short prayer each day.
